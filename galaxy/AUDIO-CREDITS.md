# Audio credits

“Also sprach Zarathustra” (Introduction / Sunrise), composed by Richard Strauss.
This application includes a recording by Kevin MacLeod, sourced from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Richard_Strauss_-_Also_Sprach_Zarathustra.ogg)
under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The Wikimedia transcoded MP3 is used without musical changes. It is a separate
recording, not the film soundtrack master.

The cockpit laser, impact, explosion and warning sounds are synthesized in the
browser by `soundtrack.js` and use no third-party recordings.
